Crate Stack Port

An engine rebuilt from the decompiled phone firmware. It loads sprites, the start picture and texts from out/stack/ (made by scripts/extract/extract_stack.py from your own dump). Hold 4/6 (or ←/→) walk & push, 1 2 3 (or Q W E / ↑) jump, 0 pause + score, * sound, F1/F2 soft keys, Esc end.
