Box Pusher Port

An engine rebuilt from the decompiled phone firmware logic. It loads levels and tiles from out/mtb/levels.json + tiles.json (made by scripts/extract/extract_mtb.py from your own dump). Default keys: 2 4 6 8/arrows move, Q left soft (undo), W right soft (reset), * sound, Esc exit. Remap them under Controls in the menu; pick a starting level with Level.
