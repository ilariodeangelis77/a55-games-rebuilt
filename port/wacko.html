Whack

Hit the critters as they pop out of the nine holes, and spare the bunnies. Rules, timings and sprites come from the decompiled firmware.

Controls

1–9

◤ soft soft ◥ OK

*

End

These are phone keys; you choose which keyboard keys press them.
